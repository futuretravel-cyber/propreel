# PropReel — full product & UX review

**Date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** the fixes marked ✅ below were applied in the follow-up pass on 23 October 2026; items without ✅ remain open (they are the larger refactors).
**Scope:** marketing site, auth, dashboard, all AI tools, credits, billing, payment, admin, design system, and the API contracts they depend on.
**Method:** read the source (frontend + `api/` + `lib/` + `modal/` docs), traced every user-facing cost, price and credit rule to the constant the server charges with. No browser walkthrough was performed, so anything marked **(verify)** is a code-reading conclusion, not an observed one.

Headline: the product is feature-rich and the engineering underneath is more careful than most — server-priced jobs, honest render progress, refunds on failure. The weaknesses are almost all at the **edges**: what the user is told before they spend, what happens when something goes wrong, and the fact that four different parts of the UI disagree about the same number.

## Applied in the 23 Oct pass

- Payment: receipt now shows the real ZAR charge; plan purchases confirm the plan; no forced 3.5s redirect; per-credit rate and annual total shown.
- Credits: allowances read from `PLANS`; "From R75"; one gift flow (deep-linked into Billing); expiry copy from `lib/creditPolicy.ts`.
- Credit rules centralised and reused by Credits, Pricing, Payment, Help/FAQ, Cancel and Refund Policy.
- Settings: profile save wired to `/api/users?action=update`; the fake password form replaced with the reset-link flow.
- Video wizard: defaults to Essential when the balance cannot afford Social; 90s→Essential crash clamped; per-render cost summary; duplicate music picker, no-op orientation Clear and admin-facing copy removed.
- Billing: dunning route fixed; totals follow filters; status labels; current plan disabled; plan name lookup; View All target; "AI Avatar" instead of "FlashHead".
- Public site: real `/faq` page (shared with the Help modal); footer link fixed; homepage free-credit promise and tier names corrected.
- Pricing: dark shell, annual charge disclosed, R75 minimum, 30-day retention card.
- Descriptions: Property24 and Private Property exports are now different layouts; address-or-photo required before charging.
- Social: real platform limits (Instagram 2,200; X 280; LinkedIn 3,000) instead of invented ones.
- Accessibility: `ink-500` contrast raised to ~4.6:1; reduced-motion support (CSS + `MotionConfig`); skip link works on public pages; progress announcements no longer chatter; Workbench progress bar given its own row.
- Removed the artificial 300–450 ms `useSimulatedLoading` delays.

## The five things to fix first

1. **The free-tier promise breaks on the default path.** Signup gives 5 credits (`SIGNUP_BONUS`). The homepage says "your 5 free credits cover your first 30-second reel" — true only for **Essential 30s (5 credits)**. But `VideoTab` defaults to `tier = 'SOCIAL'`, which is **34 credits** for 30s. A brand-new user opens the Video Generator, sees "Generate Video (34 credits)", and cannot proceed. Either default the tier to Essential while `credits < 34`, or state the promise precisely ("one Essential 30s reel") and surface Essential as the default "start here" tier.
2. **The credits page computes allowances from stale numbers.** `CreditSystemDashboard` hardcodes `STARTER 115 / PROFESSIONAL 600 / PREMIUM 1300 / AGENCY 5000`. `PLANS` says **110 / 520 / 1150 / 4300**. Every plan's progress bar and "x / N monthly allowance" is wrong, and on Professional/Premium the bar can exceed 100%. Derive from `PLANS`, not a literal.
3. **Three contradictory stories about credit expiry.** In one session a user can read: "Credits expire after 365 days" (top-up), "Never expire as long as account is active" (expiry card), "Purchased credit bundles last 12 months" (help), and for subscription credits both "roll over to next month" and "expire at next renewal date" in the *same sentence*. Pick one rule per credit type, put it in one constant, and render it everywhere.
4. **Two dead controls that look like they work.** Settings → Profile has "Save Changes" and "Update Password" buttons with no handlers, and the Phone field is never persisted. Users will believe their details were saved. Either wire them or remove the tab until it works.
5. **The payment success screen shows credits as rand.** `PaymentPage` sets `amount = topupAmount` for top-ups and prints `formatZar(amount)` on the success card: buying 100 credits (R150) congratulates you with "Amount R100". Use the same conversion as the checkout summary (`topupAmount × CREDIT_PACK_RAND`).

### Copy that erodes trust

- **"Credits are non-refundable once spent."** Reads as though unspent credits are refundable, which they are not. Say what you mean: "Renders are non-refundable once they start. If a render fails, credits are returned automatically."
- **"Charged only when a render succeeds"** on the Description/Social cost badges. The mint charges first and refunds on failure. Use: "Charged when you generate; refunded automatically if it fails."
- **"Purchased credits never expire (365 days of inactivity)."** A 365-day expiry is an expiry. Suggested: "Top-up credits stay until you use them. They expire only after 365 days with no account activity."
- **The pricing page adds a fourth version**: "Purchased credits: Valid for 365 days after purchase" — a straight 365-day expiry, not an inactivity rule. This is the most prominent of all the statements and the least compatible with the others.
- **"Unused credits roll over to next month. Expire at next renewal date."** Pick one. If rollover is real, drop the second sentence.
- **Gift Credits appears twice** (Credits quick action and Billing tab), with two different implementations and defaults. Keep one.
- **Auto Top-up**: the field is credits but the help text says "min R50". Also "You'll receive an email notification before each charge" — confirm `lib/email.ts` actually sends one; if not, delete the promise.

### Missing cost information

- **The Video wizard never totals the job.** Generate shows `config + avatar`, but the AI Write charge (1–3), per-clip re-rolls, and the avatar sub-step are separate spends. Add a persistent "This video" strip: `Tier 34 ⚡ · Script 1 ⚡ · Avatar 8 ⚡ = 43 ⚡ ≈ R64.50`, updating as choices change.
- **The billing "Credit Cost Breakdown"** shows only `configurations[0].credits` with no duration, so "Social 30" is unreadable. Show the full matrix (30/60/90) or label the row "Social · 30s".
- **Bundles have no value signal**: 50/100/250/500 at a flat R1.50 each. Either add volume discounts or stop calling them "bundles" (they are top-ups).
- **30-day media retention is absent from marketing and checkout.** Renders and AI Avatar clips are deleted after 30 days (`RETENTION_DAYS`), stated in the Studio header and Privacy page only. An agent buying a plan reasonably assumes their portfolio is hosted. Put it on the pricing page, in the welcome modal, and next to Download.
- **VAT**: "All prices include VAT" appears on checkout and the footer, but invoices and the bundle cards never show the VAT line. A B2B buyer needs subtotal/VAT/total.
- **Annual pricing** has no equivalent monthly saving shown in the plan cards (it exists in `PLANS.annualZar`). "R1 270/mo or R1 200/mo billed annually" would convert better than a hidden annual path.

## 2. Broken or dead UI

- `/#faq` is linked from the public footer and the Help modal, but **no FAQ section exists on the homepage** — the link scrolls nowhere. `HelpModal` already contains a good FAQ dataset; render it as a real `/faq` page (also good for SEO).
- **Skip link** in `App.tsx` targets `#main-content`, which only exists inside `DashboardLayout`. On every marketing page it does nothing.
- **Theme toggle** in `PublicLayout` switches a theme the public site ignores (`bg-ink-950 dark:bg-ink-950`, `text-white`). Either remove the toggle on public pages or finish the light theme.

- **Music step duplicates the picker**: a select in the music block, then a second select inside "Enhancements". One control.
- **"Your administrator can upload tracks in Admin → Music Library"** is shown to ordinary agents. Internal instruction leaking into customer UI.
- **Settings → Profile** dead buttons (see top-five).
- **`/dashboard/bundles`** route missing (see table).
- **`useSimulatedLoading(300/400/450)`** deliberately delays skeletons on every navigation to Overview, Projects, Photo Editor, Description, Social and Video. This makes the app feel slower than it is. Delete it; skeletons should only cover real fetches.

## 3. Copy, page by page

### Homepage (`src/components/HomePage.tsx`)

- **"One Studio. Seven AI Tools." — the grid below lists six**, and the floating badges list four. There are actually eight tools (Description, Photo Editor, Staging, Removal, Twilight, Social, Video, Avatar) plus a hidden Budget Video page. Count them once from a data array.
- **"Pick Your Tier: Social, Essential, Professional or Cinematic."** "Professional" is a *plan*, not a video tier. The tiers are Essential, Social, Cinematic, Premium (+ Budget). Same error in the property showcase? No — that one is correct.
- **"Social tier reel · 512p" is correct** (`videoTiers` gives Social 512p) — it was the README's 480p table that was stale. Fixed by regenerating the README rather than the page.
- **"AI Video Tiers 4"** is accurate for the picker (it filters out `BUDGET`), but the Budget studio exists at `/dashboard/budget-video` with no nav entry and no marketing mention. Promote it or remove it.
- **Hero video** is a 2.6 MB autoplay loop behind a blur — heavy for South African mobile data, for content that is 80% obscured. Use a poster image and load the video on interaction, or a much smaller/greyer loop.
- **No FAQ, no testimonial fallback.** `SocialProof` renders `null` with zero reviews, so the page jumps from tool grid to CTA. Pre-seed three real reviews (with permission) or replace the section with outcomes/metrics.
- **"About ten minutes, start to finish"** is a strong claim when render queues are the long pole. If a typical render is 4–8 minutes, say "upload in five minutes, render while you make coffee".
- **Missing from the page entirely:** the free-tier limits, the 30-day retention, whether videos are watermark-free, and what happens to your data. One 3-line "The fine print, plainly" strip near the final CTA would answer the five questions every agent asks before signing up.

Suggested hero sub-line (keeps the promise honest):
> Turn property photos into narrated listing reels in minutes. Start with 5 free credits — enough for one Essential 30-second reel, no card needed.

### Pricing & plan cards

- Plan features are truncated to three per card in billing (`.slice(0, 3)`), chosen arbitrarily; Premium's most persuasive line can vanish.
- "Switch Plan" on the current plan's card should read "Current plan" and be disabled, not a button to the same plan's checkout.
- **Annual billing doesn't say what will be charged.** The toggle exists and shows `annualZar` as "/month" (Starter R110), but checkout charges `annualZar × 12` (R1 320). Add "billed R1 320 per year" under the monthly figure.
- **The pricing page is a light island inside a dark site.** `PricingPage` renders `bg-gradient-to-b from-purple-50 to-white` with `text-gray-900` cards inside `PublicLayout`, whose root is `bg-ink-950 text-white` with a dark glass navbar. The transition from dark homepage → light pricing → dark checkout is jarring, and `badge-brand` (`text-brand-300` on 20% brand) is nearly invisible on the white "Most Popular" card. Either make pricing dark like the rest of the funnel, or give it a proper light shell (navbar included).
- The pricing footer says "min R150" (`50 × CREDIT_PACK_RAND`) while the Credits page says "From R50". Both should read "minimum 50 credits (R75)".

### Auth

- The trial is opt-in on signup but is never explained in one sentence ("Try everything free for 7 days — we'll remind you before it ends"). The dashboard then explains it in three separate banners (`DashboardLayout`, `TrialDeadlineModal`, `EnhancedBilling`). One banner, one modal, one truth.
- Signup with `?plan=` should carry through to checkout; today `/register?plan=X&cycle=Y&price=Z` is built by `PaymentPage`, but the top-up path drops `amount`, so a signed-out buyer who creates an account loses their chosen bundle.

### Payment page

- Headline "Complete Your Subscription" also appears for plans the user has already got; "Top Up Credits" is fine.
- Unknown/missing `plan` renders "Subscribe to undefined" (`plan?.name` interpolation).
- No order line for the per-credit rate on top-ups, and no VAT split.
- The 3.5-second auto-redirect to the dashboard fires while the user is still reading their receipt. Remove the auto-redirect, or make it 10 seconds with a visible countdown.
- "Credit Policy" paragraph mixes refunds, cancellation and expiry into one run-on sentence. Split into two bullets.

### Property Description

- **"Download for Property24" and "Download for Private Property" produce byte-identical text** with different filenames. Either implement the portal-specific formats (Property24: no markdown, shorter; Private Property: different structure) or collapse to one "Download .txt" and one "Copy".
- Generate is enabled with an empty form; the model then invents a property. Require at least address + property type (or generate from photos explicitly, as the Video tab does).
- "Render as video" jumps tabs with no confirmation of the extra credits it will spend.

### Social Media

- The wizard has four steps but only **Suburb** is required; the error "Complete all four steps before generating a post" is therefore misleading. Say "Add a suburb to continue" at the point of failure.
- **Platform character limits look wrong** (`src/lib/constants.ts`): Instagram 300 (real limit ~2 200), X 240 (~280), LinkedIn 1 000 (~3 000), Facebook 500. These caps force agents to cut copy that would have fitted. Verify against current limits and label them "recommended" rather than "limit".
- "Platform-optimised posts with strict char limits" on the homepage promises something the limits currently get wrong.

### Video wizard

- **Step 1 is labelled "Description" but contains no description field** — it is the listing-details form. Rename to "Listing details".
- The overrun warning says "the last frame will stay frozen ~4s past the end". Accurate, but "frozen" reads as a bug. Try: "Your narration runs 4s longer than the video, so the final frame holds a little longer. Trim to ~72 words for a clean end."
- Interpolated internal terms: "reserving credits", "job_token", "worker" (in error copy). Users should see "holding your credits" and "our renderer".
- 12 photos × (Interior/Exterior + camera move) = up to 24 taps before you can continue. Add: default all to Interior + Zoom in, a "same as previous" action, and an "Apply to all" for the last set.

### Avatar (FlashHead)

- Removing the photo here **also clears it in the Video tab**. The ✕ doesn't say so; add a confirm or microcopy ("This also removes the photo from the Video step").
- "Regenerate (8 credits)" is good; add the same clarity to the initial CTA ("Generate AI Avatar Video · 8 ⚡ ≈ R12").
- The panel never states that the clip is deletable after 30 days — link the retention notice inline.

### Admin

- Tab names assume insider knowledge: **"P&L"**, **"Ops"**, **"Prompts"**, **"Update Mode"**. "Profit & loss", "Render operations", "AI prompt library", "Maintenance mode" cost nothing and remove a lookup.
- The whole panel is one `card-dark` with tables inside; a sticky sub-header and per-tab description line ("Who is spending, and on what") would orient a new admin.
- No global search/filter across users or agencies; no CSV export anywhere, which is the first thing a finance person asks for.
- `AdminAgencies` authorises by passing the admin's email in the query string. Authorisation must come from the session, never a client-supplied identifier **(security, verify server-side)**.
- `FalHealthCard` is genuinely useful. Extend the same pattern to Paystack, S3 and the worker queue so "is the product up?" is one screen.

## 4. Information architecture & navigation

- **The same route has four names.** "Create" (mobile), "Studio" (marketing/blog), "AI Video Generator" (page title), "Video Studio" (footer). Pick one product noun — "Studio" — and use it everywhere.
- **Two navigation systems with different taxonomies**: the sidebar (business → create → library → account) and the bottom mobile bar (Dashboard / Create / Projects / Credits / Profile). The mobile bar's "Create" is aria-labelled "AI tools" and hides Photo Editor, Descriptions and Social entirely.
- **Hidden route:** Budget Video exists at `/dashboard/budget-video` but has no nav entry, no marketing mention, and its own 5-step wizard. Either promote it (as "Budget" inside the tier picker) or remove it.
- **Credits and Billing are two tabs on one page**, but the bell, the profile menu, the low-credit banner and the dunning email all link to different combinations of `?tab=`. Consolidate: one "Billing" destination, deep links preserved.
- **Notifications** live inside Settings as a tab while also having a bell icon; that is fine, but the tab should be called "Notifications", not "Settings → Notifications", in the bell tooltip.
- **Suggested sidebar grouping** (if not already grouped): Create (Studio, Avatar, Photo Editor, Description, Social) · Library (Projects, Listings, Branding) · Insight (Analytics, Tutorials) · Account (Credits & billing, Settings, Agency) · Admin (only for admins).
- **Missing global "New" action.** From any page, an agent should be able to start a render in one click. A persistent "＋ New video" in the header (or a command palette action) beats hunting the sidebar.

## 5. Design system: colour, type, motion

### Colour

- **Three purples in one product.** `--color-brand-700 = #800080` (CSS "purple"), buttons gradient `#800080 → #9a35a6 → #b252bd`, while components sprinkle Tailwind `purple-400/500/600/700/950` and `fuchsia-400`. `#800080` is a muddy, low-chroma purple next to `#a855f7`. Choose one ramp (the Tailwind `purple`/`violet` ramp is already used in 80% of the UI) and make `brand-*` alias it; the gradient then becomes a deliberate accent rather than a fourth hue.
- **Status colour is inconsistent**: success green, warning amber, danger red are defined as tokens but many screens use `yellow-400`, `amber-300`, `red-300`, `green-400` interchangeably. Low-credit = yellow in one component, amber in another, blue for "info".
- **The Credits quick actions** (green / blue / pink / orange gradient tiles) are the loudest thing on the page and none of them is the primary action. They break the brand system and compete with "Upgrade". Make Top Up the single primary card and demote the rest to ghost buttons.
- **Emoji as iconography**: ⚡, ⏳, 🏡, 🎬, ✨, 💡 mixed with Lucide icons. ⚡ in particular is used both as an emoji and as the `Zap` icon for the same concept. Standardise on `Zap` for credits.
- **Contrast:** `--color-ink-500` (#5b678b) on `--color-ink-950` (#0a0d18) is ≈3.3:1. It is used for 10–12px helper text throughout (`text-ink-500`, `text-[10px]`). That fails WCAG AA for body text. Bump `ink-500` to ~#6b7899 or stop using it below 14px. `ink-400` (≈5.6:1) is fine.
- **Light theme is half-built**: `.card-light`, `.input-light`, `.glass-light`, `.label-light`, `.btn-quiet` exist but the app is dark-only. Ship the theme or delete the classes — half-built theming is how unreadable states get shipped later.

### Type & layout

- Headings are uniformly `font-black` with tight tracking; combined with `text-balance` on the hero it works, but every page opens with the same 2rem bold headline and one line of `text-ink-400`. Section hierarchy would improve with a smaller, muted eyebrow above each H1 (as the admin panel now has) and more space between sections than inside them.
- `.label` is uppercase 12px with `letter-spacing: 0.05em`; long labels ("Photos for AI to describe (2 / 4)") read as shouting. Use sentence case for form labels, keep uppercase for section eyebrows only.
- Cards are `rounded-2xl`/`rounded-3xl` inconsistently (`card-dark` = 1rem, marketing = 1.5–2.5rem). Two radii are enough.

### Motion

- Every `Reveal` animates, plus perpetual `repeat: Infinity` floating badges, `animate-pulse-soft` CTAs and `animate-float`. There is **no `prefers-reduced-motion` handling anywhere**. Add a global reduce block and tone the infinite loops down; perpetual motion on a page someone is trying to read is the most common accessibility complaint.
- The CTA pulse is used on two buttons simultaneously in the hero and again in the final CTA — if everything pulses, nothing is emphasised.

## 6. Accessibility

- Skip link broken on public pages (above).
- Icon-only buttons are mostly labelled — keep that discipline on the new `ImageWorkbench` re-render button (currently a bare `RefreshCw`).
- Form inputs are frequently unlabelled by association: `<label class="label">Price</label><input>` with no `htmlFor`/`id`, and several selects rely on `aria-label` only. Screen readers announce no field name. Systematic fix: a `<Field label>` wrapper.
- Error text is often colour + icon only, without `role="alert"`; the description/social error toasts are fine, but the inline credit errors are not announced.
- The `RenderProgressBar` has `aria-live="polite"` on a wrapper that updates every second — that will chatter. Move `aria-live` to the stage text only, and drop the elapsed-time updates from the live region.
- Modal focus trapping: verify `OnboardingTutorial`, `HelpModal`, `TrialDeadlineModal` and `LowCreditNudge` trap Tab and restore focus on close. `LowCreditNudge` handles Escape (good) but not focus restoration.
- Touch targets: most are `min-h-[44px]` (good); the ✕ remove-photo buttons are 24px and only appear on hover — on touch they are effectively invisible. Show them always on mobile.

## 7. Performance & frontend architecture

- **`src/App.tsx` is 6 248 lines** and contains the dashboard shell, six tool pages, the 1 500-line video wizard, the budget wizard, the admin panel and the route table. Route-level `lazy()` exists but everything in App ships in the initial chunk. Split at least: `VideoTab`, `BudgetVideoTab`, `AdminPage` + admin tabs, `DescriptionTab`, `SocialTab`, and `DashboardLayout`.
- **Hero video** (2.6 MB) and the demo reel (864 KB) load on the landing page; the demo reel is already lazy, the hero isn't.
- **Charts** (`recharts`) load on Overview and Billing; both are behind lazy boundaries (billing is, overview isn't).
- **`useSimulatedLoading`** adds 300–450 ms to navigations by design.
- **Images**: most marketing images have `srcSet` (good); the bento cards use a single 800/1200 fixed size — add `srcSet` there too.
- **No offscreen `content-visibility`** on long marketing sections; a cheap win on mid-range Android.

## 8. Backend, data and documentation

- **`README.md` is materially wrong** about plans, endpoints and credit expiry. It documents `POST /api/credits/deduct`, `/api/subscriptions/create`, `/api/paystack/initialize`; the app actually calls query-style handlers (`/api/credits?action=…`, `/api/subscriptions?action=create`, `/api/paystack/initialize` exists but with `credits`/`user_id` bodies). Anyone integrating against the README will fail. Regenerate the API section from the code or delete it.
- **Plan numbers exist in at least four places** (`PLANS`, `CreditSystemDashboard`, `README`, marketing copy about Premium credits) — this is the same class of bug as the video tier prices were. Single source: `PLANS` + a test that asserts the rendered strings contain its numbers.
- **Credit expiry rules** are duplicated across `CreditSystemDashboard`, `EnhancedBilling`, `HelpModal`, `RefundPolicyPage`, `CancelSubscriptionPage` and `PaymentPage`. Centralise into `lib/creditPolicy.ts` and render from it.
- **Authorisation by email query param** in admin fetches (`?action=admin_agencies&email=…`, `?action=admin_ledger&email=…`) should be replaced by session-derived identity. Even if the server verifies the email matches the session, it teaches future code the wrong pattern.
- **Silent failure on project finalisation**: `finalizeRender` calls `updateProject(...).catch(() => {})`. If that write fails, the user sees a video but the project stays `PROCESSING` and the nightly reaper may later mark it failed/refund. Surface a retry or a background reconciliation.
- **Media retention** (30 days) is enforced server-side and disclosed in Privacy, but nowhere in the product's value proposition. Add it to the pricing page and to the download CTA ("Download now — renders are kept 30 days").
- **Observability**: `FalHealthCard` is the only health surface. Add a `/status` feed that reflects real probes (it exists as a page — make sure it reads live probes rather than marketing copy).

## 9. Error and empty states

Good already: the payment-verify failure card ("your payment is safe, we just couldn't confirm it yet" + Check again), the render-error refund messaging, the per-widget `ErrorBoundary`.

Gaps:

- **Generic "Payment failed"** on checkout init hides the real cause (missing card, Paystack down, plan invalid). Pass the server message.
- **Empty states**: Billing has no empty state for invoices, no "first render" onboarding in Projects beyond a button, and the Overview charts show "No credit usage" without a CTA. Each empty state should end in the one action that fills it.
- **404 (`NotFoundPage`)** should offer Search + the three most likely destinations, not just a link home.
- **Offline**: `OfflineBanner` exists — make sure queued uploads state their recovery ("3 photos will upload when you're back online").
- **Partial uploads**: the queue parks on 429 and retries (good) but the user can navigate away with no persistent indicator except a toast. A small fixed "Uploading 2 of 5…" pill in the header would remove the anxiety.
